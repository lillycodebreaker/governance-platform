Meeting Summary — AI Governance: Archer IRQ Automation with Temporal Workflows

Meeting focus: Demonstration of an automated workflow that integrates Archer IRQ submissions with Quickbase AI Governance registration, using Temporal orchestration, GenAI-assisted form prefill, automated follow-ups, and human-in-the-loop reviews.

Overall status: The solution has been demonstrated locally, with end-to-end testing underway in QA. Production deployment is pending further testing and code review.

1. Key Discussion Notes

A. Current Challenges — Manual IRQ Processing

The existing process requires substantial manual effort from the AI Governance team.

* Business owners submit IRQs (introduction requests) through Archer.
* AI Governance manually reviews IRQs to identify AI and GenAI use cases.
* The team contacts business owners to complete additional Quickbase registration forms.
* Business owners must re-enter information already provided in Archer.
* Governance reviewers manually follow up on incomplete submissions.
* Growing AI adoption is increasing workload and processing delays.

Business objective: Reduce duplicate data entry, eliminate unnecessary manual follow-ups, and automate the registration workflow while maintaining human accountability.

B. Proposed Solution — Temporal-Based Automation

Archer IRQ submissions

Source system

Temporal synchronization workflow

Detect new records · Store in MongoDB · Identify AI use cases

Per-record workflow

Resolve owner · Check duplicates · Generate prefilled forms

Business owner review

Email link · Verify data · Complete missing fields · Submit

Quickbase AI Governance registration

Confirm submission · Notify owner · Ready for governance review

Governance dashboard monitors status, reminders, escalations, and manual interventions throughout the process.

C. Two Main Temporal Workflows

Workflow	Responsibility
IRQ Sync Workflow	Periodically retrieves Archer records, stores them in MongoDB, detects new IRQs, and initiates processing
Individual Record Workflow	Checks AI relevance, identifies the owner, prevents duplicate submissions, prefills forms, sends notifications, and tracks completion

Technical implementation highlights:

* Sync can run once or twice daily; twice daily was the expected initial cadence.
* Archer data is processed in pages of 100 records to manage payload size and recovery.
* MongoDB watermarks allow synchronization to resume from the last processed position.
* Each record has an independent workflow, allowing concurrent processing.
* Individual workflows remain active while awaiting submission.
* Temporal manages workflow state, scheduling, reminders, and escalations.

D. GenAI-Assisted Form Prefill

The workflow reuses Archer information and employs some LLM assistance to populate the appropriate Quickbase forms.

During the demo:

* The first GenAI form contained 41 fields, with 17 requiring user input after prefill.
* The second AI Use Case Inventory form required zero additional fields in the demonstrated example because information was reused from Archer and the first form.
* The business owner reviewed and submitted both forms.
* Generated Quickbase IDs and submission confirmation were displayed.

Expected benefit: Significantly reduce manual data entry and improve submission accuracy and consistency.

E. Human-in-the-Loop Controls

Two human checkpoints were demonstrated.

Business owner review: Users verify all prefilled values, complete missing fields, and explicitly submit their forms. Automated prefill does not bypass owner approval.

AI Governance intervention: The governance team can monitor processing, resolve unknown requestors, reassign ownership, resend notifications, escalate records, and manually trigger workflows through the dashboard.

This maintains accountability while reducing routine administrative work.

F. Monitoring Dashboard and Architecture

The Angular-based dashboard provides visibility into:

Capability	Description
Synchronization monitoring	Last sync, recent activity, and scheduled execution visibility
Record status	Processing, awaiting owner action, completed, or exception states
Workflow tracking	Individual record progress and failure details
Manual interventions	Reassignment, restart or trigger, email resend, and escalation
SLA monitoring	Highlight submissions that exceed the expected response window, discussed as approximately 3 days
Audit visibility	Review the workflow history and actions without direct Temporal access

The architecture integrates Temporal, Archer, MongoDB, Quickbase, SMTP email, and an Angular dashboard with FastAPI/proxy integration.

A technical challenge was exposing Temporal workflow operations through the existing proxy infrastructure. Additional methods were implemented to support dashboard actions.

6. Key Takeaways for Leadership

The team demonstrated a promising approach for automating the Archer IRQ-to-Quickbase AI Governance registration process, significantly reducing repetitive data entry and manual follow-up activities.

The solution uses Temporal to manage long-running workflows, GenAI-assisted form prefill to reduce business-owner effort, and an operational dashboard to preserve governance oversight and human intervention.

Immediate priority: Complete load testing, resolve outstanding technical issues, and obtain engineering approval for production deployment.

Strategic opportunity: Reuse this orchestration pattern across the broader AI Governance platform, including registration, risk assessment, evidence collection, approval routing, and lifecycle monitoring.

Recommended leadership focus: In addition to production readiness, establish measurable outcomes such as registration turnaround time, automation success rate, manual follow-up reduction, exception rate, and business-owner completion time. These measures will help demonstrate the business value of the automation and guide further investment.
