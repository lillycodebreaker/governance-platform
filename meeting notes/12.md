6. Key Takeaways for Leadership

1. Prepare a clear platform value story. The leadership walkthrough should explain the legacy challenges, improvements delivered, and measurable benefits.

2. Quickbase migration will be incremental. Frontend modernization can proceed while backend automation remains in place, minimizing disruption.

3. Pilot stability remains a priority. Feature flags, regression testing, and user feedback should guide upcoming releases.

4. Clarify business requirements before starting new forms. The third-party AI exception request requires additional discovery to avoid creating redundant workflows.

5. Strengthen engineering delivery practices. Clear Jira stories, requirements confirmation, and stakeholder signoff will become increasingly important as the team grows.

6. Improve institutional knowledge. Updated onboarding documentation and shared technical resources will reduce ramp-up time for new engineers.

7. Recommended Immediate Leadership Focus

The most useful near-term deliverables are:

* Leadership Comparison Package: Current versus future registration process, architecture changes, pain points addressed, and available business outcome measures.
* November Engineering Delivery Plan: Migration milestones, pilot enhancements, Quickbase frontend work, and third-party automation dependencies.
* Requirements and Delivery Framework: A consistent process for documenting decisions, confirming requirements with Governance, and maintaining Jira priorities.

Overall recommendation: Use the upcoming leadership review to demonstrate the value already delivered, while establishing stronger requirements management and execution discipline for the next phase of AI Governance modernization.
