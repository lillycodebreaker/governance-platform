5. My Engineering Leadership Priorities

The meetings consistently identified five areas where I can add value.

A. Establish delivery discipline. Every major initiative should have clear requirements, Jira stories, acceptance criteria, owners, dependencies, and business signoff.

B. Improve architecture and automation. Reuse existing technology, integrate through APIs, avoid duplicate systems, and design workflows that minimize manual steps.

C. Strengthen production operations. Establish a predictable release process, testing, incident handling, ServiceNow ticket visibility, and ongoing support.

D. Build auditability into the platform. Capture decisions, evidence, control assessments, reviewer overrides, and lifecycle changes for regulatory and internal review.

E. Demonstrate business value. Automatically report governance turnaround time, review workload, automation savings, and system performance.
